Is a D2C fashion brand building a loyal customer base, or renting revenue with discounts?

THE CATCH
Every variable pair has |r| < 0.05. Loyalty can't be discovered in this data; it has to be constructed from transparent rules.

VERDICT
A thin but real loyal core (7%), and margin given away on 43% of customers for zero measurable return.
North-star metric: full-price revenue share, 57% today. The strategy works if it rises while total revenue holds.
RECOMMENDATIONS
▸ Sunset promotions in two phases; never discount the loyal core.
▸ Acquire Ideal-Customer lookalikes on behaviour, not demographics.
▸ Invest in organic-demand states.
▸ Service-recover the 680 at-risk customers with support, not discounts.
RULES OF PLAY
▸ One snapshot, no timestamps: retention can't be measured directly.
▸ Rule-based segments, documented so they can be challenged.
▸ No margin or cost data: margin given away is inferred.
▸ Likely synthetic data: the method transfers better than the numbers.
