THE AI OPERATING SYSTEM FOR TRAVEL AGENCIES

One multi-tenant workspace where AI agents draft itineraries, marketing creatives, vendor deals and lead follow-ups, and a human approves every step before anything goes out.

Built at Mount Diggers for small agencies where one or two people do everything by hand: itineraries, posters, chasing hotels, posting, and then the flood of leads.

DESIGN RULE
Every agent only drafts. No message is sent, no post is published and no vendor is emailed until a human clicks approve.

CORE ATENANT ISOLATION

Every business table carries a tenant_id, and Postgres Row-Level Security filters rows by the tenant bound to the request. The API connects as a non-superuser role with FORCE ROW LEVEL SECURITY, so even a buggy query can't cross tenants.
