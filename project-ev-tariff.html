AGENTIC DYNAMIC TARIFF OPTIMIZATION FOR EV CHARGING

A self-improving pricing engine for EV charging stations: three cooperating agents forecast demand, set a dynamic ₹/kWh tariff and learn from simulated outcomes.

Built on real Caltech ACN-Data sessions and cross-checked against the Shenzhen UrbanEV network.

HONEST SCOPE
Demand response is assumed, not estimated: revenue, wait-time and uplift figures are simulation-based counterfactuals, not observed outcomes.

VERDICT
+8.2% revenue and −22% peak wait at about the same average price, in simulation.
Demand is workplace-shaped and station utilization is low (median 6%), so the biggest opportunity is filling idle hours, not handling over-capacity.

RULES OF PLAY
▸ Train/test split by time: no peeking at the future.
▸ Any policy that loses revenue is penalized.
▸ Deterministic: RANDOM_STATE = 42, no external LLM or API.
▸ Prices use the brief's ₹15/kWh, not a USD→INR conversion.
